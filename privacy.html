Privacy policy

Brain · last updated 5 October 2026

Who uses Brain

Brain is a private app with a single user: the owner of this GitHub account. It is not offered to anyone else and does not accept sign-ups.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Brain reads his own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, and can send email and create calendar events on his behalf after he approves them.

Where the data goes

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. Parts of it may be processed by the AI assistant the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude), solely to help him with his own tasks.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Brain's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Brain's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local data from his computer.
